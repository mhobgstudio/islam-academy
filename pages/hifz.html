🧠 Hifz Planner

Memorization tracker using the classic 3-tier system: new lesson (sabaq), recent revision (sabqi), older revision (manzil).

The daily method

• Sabaq (new): memorize ~5 lines / half a page daily. Read each ayah 10× looking, then 10× from memory.
• Sabqi (recent): recite the last 7 days of lessons to a partner or recording.
• Manzil (old): cycle everything you have memorized — e.g. one juz per day.
• Recite NEW lessons in your 5 daily prayers to lock them in.
