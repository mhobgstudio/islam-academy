🎵 Tajweed Rules

The science of beautiful, correct recitation (Hafs an Asim). Study a rule, then hear it applied in the reader.

Before you recite

أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ istiʿādha — seeking refuge
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ basmalah — at surah start (except Surah 9)

Practice loop

1. Read the rule → 2. Open the reader on a short surah → 3. Recite aloud applying it → 4. Listen to Alafasy reciting the same ayah and compare.
